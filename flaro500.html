Make it through all 4 weeks and you’re in the draw to win $500. Free to enter. The full terms below are what count.

2. Who can enter

4. How to enter

Download Flaro from the App Store, create an account, and join the group called Flaro 500 before entries close. Anyone who joins after entries close cannot qualify.

5. How to qualify for the draw

  • In each of the 4 weeks, complete a workout on at least 4 different days and post proof of each one to the Flaro 500 challenge in the app. Only one workout per day counts.
  • A workout means a genuine session of physical exercise of at least 20 minutes — for example the gym, a run, a class, yoga, swimming, a sport, or a brisk walk.
  • Each proof must be a photo taken with the Flaro in-app camera at the time of the workout, and must reasonably show the workout (for example you at the gym, on your run, or your activity). Photos from your camera roll, old photos, or other people’s photos do not count.
  • Proof must be posted on the day of the workout. Missed days cannot be made up later, and streak freezes or other in-app items do not count towards the competition.
  • Miss the 4-workout target in any week and you are out of the draw. There are no second chances.
  • Days are counted using the date on your phone in the app, as shown in the Flaro 500 group.

6. Bonus entries

During the competition we may announce optional weekly bonus challenges in the app and on our social media. Each bonus challenge you complete, with proof taken in the in-app camera during the stated time, earns you one extra entry into the draw, up to a maximum of 4 bonus entries. Bonus entries only count if you qualify for the draw under clause 5. Bonus challenges are optional and never knock you out of the competition.

7. Checking proof and disqualification

  • We may review any proof at any time. We may reject proof that does not meet these terms, and a rejected day does not count.
  • We may disqualify anyone we reasonably believe has posted fake, copied, misleading or inappropriate proof, used more than one account, interfered with the competition, or broken Flaro’s Terms of Service.
  • Before the winner is confirmed, we will check all of their proof. If it does not meet these terms, they will be disqualified and another entry will be drawn.
  • Our decision on eligibility and the winner is final, and no correspondence will be entered into.

8. The prize

One winner receives $500 (AUD), paid by bank transfer to an Australian bank account in the winner’s name. The prize is not transferable. Total prize value: $500.

9. The draw

  • The draw will be held on Tuesday 17 November 2026 in Queensland, Australia, by random selection from all valid entries (including bonus entries). It will be recorded on video.
  • Your chance of winning depends on how many valid entries there are.

10. Contacting and announcing the winner

  • We will contact the winner through the Flaro app and by SMS to the phone number on their Flaro account within 2 days of the draw.
  • The winner’s Flaro display name will be announced on Flaro’s social media (including Instagram @flaro.app) by Tuesday 24 November 2026.
  • The winner must reply and provide proof of identity and age, and their bank details, within 14 days of being contacted. If they don’t, the prize will be redrawn on Wednesday 9 December 2026 at the same place, and that winner will be contacted and announced the same way within 2 days.
