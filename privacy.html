Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flaro ("Flaro", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Flaro mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We only collect what we need to run the App:

InformationWhy we collect it
Phone numberTo create your account and sign you in via a one-time SMS code.
Display name & usernameTo identify you to other members of your groups.
Profile photo (optional)Shown on your profile and posts.
Proof content (photos, videos, captions)The core feature — posted to your groups as proof you completed a challenge.
Group and challenge activityGroup memberships, challenges, completions, streaks, and XP, so the App can track progress and leaderboards.
Push notification tokenTo send reminders and notifications, if you enable them.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or payment details, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account;
  • To provide core features — posting proof, groups, challenges, streaks, and leaderboards;
  • To show your posts and profile to members of the groups you join;
  • To send notifications and reminders you've enabled;
  • To keep the App safe, including reviewing reported content and enforcing our Terms.

3. How your information is shared

  • With other members of your groups. Your name, username, profile photo, proof posts, and streaks are visible to members of groups you join. Don't post anything you wouldn't want your group to see.
  • With our service providers. We use Supabase to host our database, authentication, and media storage, and platform push services (Apple/Google) to deliver notifications. They process data only to provide these services to us.
  • For legal reasons. If required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Flaro or others.

We never sell your personal information.

4. Content moderation

The App lets users report posts and block other users. When content is reported, we may review it and the associated account to enforce our Terms of Service. Reports are stored so we can act on them.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account. Some information may remain in routine backups for a limited period or where we're required to keep it by law.

6. Security

We use reputable infrastructure and reasonable technical measures to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and restrict access to it.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

8. Your rights

You can access and update your profile in the App, delete your account and data at any time, and contact us with any privacy request. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law (such as the Australian Privacy Principles), which we will honour.

9. International users

Flaro is operated from Australia, and your information may be stored and processed in Australia or other countries where our service providers operate. By using the App you consent to this.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

11. Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Email us at info@flaro.net.au.
